## Brand & Style

The design system projects the grim, austere gravity of high-stakes realm-versus-realm medieval warfare. It serves veteran combatants, guild warlords, and competitive analysts who demand ruthless clarity, definitive rankings, and uncompromising speed.

Drawing from Dark Minimalist and Chiseled Medieval aesthetics:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian baselines, cold slate surfaces, and battle-weathered ironwork. Visual clutter is eliminated in favor of razor-sharp data legibility and disciplined contrast.
- **Antique Gold Restraint:** Gold is not ambient decor; it is earned tribute. It is reserved exclusively for the most critical action tiers, crowning tournament achievements, top-of-ladder champions, and sovereign brand marks.
- **Faction Allegiances:** Realm colors are sovereign identifiers, never broad decorative washes or background fills. They act as sharp heraldic markers cut into the dark steel canvas.
- **Tone:** Severe, commanding, precise, and permanent—reminiscent of engraved war ledgers and memorial runestones.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an abyss of warm blacks and cold charcoals, highlighted by chiseled metallic edges and strictly controlled faction accents.

- **Foundations:**
  - `surface_canvas` (`#050505`): Foundational void background.
  - `surface_raised` (`#0C0C0C`): Structural headers, table rows, and page navigation.
  - `surface_card` (`#121212`): Isolated battle stat panels and competitor cards.
- **Hairline Dividers:**
  - `border_subtle` (`#1F1F1F`): Standard grid lines, inactive inputs, and inner card borders.
  - `border_structural` (`#282828`): Exterior card outlines and modal walls.
  - `border_highlight` (`#3E3E3E`): Hover states, active tabs, and focus triggers.
- **Accents:**
  - `primary_color_hex` (`#D4AF37` Antique Gold): Reserved for Rank 1 honors, final kill confirmations, and master CTAs. Never used for large surface backgrounds or frivolous highlights.
  - Faction Colors (Syrtis `#10B981`, Ignis `#EF4444`, Alsius `#3B82F6`): Pure identity tokens used for 1px status indicators, realm insignia outlines, rank ribbons, and name badges. Never blend or gradient these together.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a strict tension between historic heraldry and relentless battlefield analytics:

- **Headlines (EB Garamond):** Used in uppercase or capitalized roman form for monumental presence. Headings evoke stone carvings, battle treatises, and classical inscriptions without sacrificing crisp rendering on modern screens.
- **Narratives & Lore (EB Garamond):** Generous line height ensures high legibility across tournament rulebooks, guild declarations, and player bios.
- **Metrics, Combat Stats, and Ledger Data (JetBrains Mono):** Dense, unambiguous, tabular-aligned figures. Used for MMR, Kill/Death ratios, realm balance percentages, and system timestamps. Numbers never shift column width during live updates.
- **Labels & Badges:** Rendered in `label-caps` (all-caps monospaced format) with extended tracking to anchor structural headers, table column titles, and faction meta-data.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column rigid desktop grid transitions to a 6-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Combat Ledger Density:** Spacing favors compact, efficient information architecture over excessive white space. Content feels fortified and structured.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, stacked leaderboard blocks).
  - Tablet: `640px – 1024px` (6 columns, `margin: 2rem`, collapsible side panels).
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, `margin: 3rem`, maximum canvas width of 1440px).
- **Tabular Rhythm:** Data rows utilize strict vertical increments of 40px or 48px to allow rapid eye tracking across wide stat tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects blurry dropshadows, glassmorphic blurs, and neon glows. Depth is communicated purely through **chiseled surfaces and structural hairline borders**.

- **Level 0 (Floor):** `#050505`. Background of the entire war room.
- **Level 1 (Trench/Card):** `#0C0C0C` background, delineated by a 1px solid outline of `#1F1F1F`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Floating Inspector):** `#121212` background, enclosed with `#282828`, featuring a 1px top border highlight of `#3E3E3E` mimicking an overhead stone light source.
- **Champion State (Gold Inscription):** When a tier or tournament node achieves championship status, the outline adopts a crisp 1px `#D4AF37` frame with a subtle, non-diffuse `0 0 0 1px #8A701E` inner bevel. No spread or blur is permitted.

## Shapes

The interface is completely non-rounded (`0px` corner radii) or clamped strictly to a maximum of `2px` for small indicators.

- **Aesthetic Principles:** Geometric, chiseled, hard-edged stone slabs. 
- **Prohibitions:** Strictly forbid pill shapes, circular chips, floating soft buttons, and bubbly SaaS radii. 
- **Cut Corners (Heraldic Notches):** Where decorative distinction is necessary on primary champion banners, diagonal 4px chamfered corners (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) may be used to evoke clipped iron plates.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA (Gold Authority):** Sharp 0px corners, background `#D4AF37`, text `#050505` (`label-caps` bold weight), border `1px solid #E5C358`. Hover: `#E2C153` with zero displacement.
- **Secondary (Iron Frame):** Background `#0C0C0C`, border `1px solid #282828`, text `#ECECEC`. Hover: border `#737373`, background `#141414`.
- **Faction Action:** Bordered in the faction's primary color with an ultra-deep tinted background (e.g., Ignis: border `#DC2626`, background `#1A0808`, text `#EF4444`).

### Stat Cards & Panels
- Constructed from `#0C0C0C` with a single 1px hairline perimeter of `#1F1F1F`.
- Inner content separation is handled through 1px horizontal dividers of `#1A1A1A` rather than loose gaps.
- Cards host monospaced metric groupings with subtle labels above bold tabular numbers.

### Leaderboard & Data Tables
- Sticky headers using `#080808` with `label-caps` text in `#5C5C5C`.
- Alternating row zebra styling is avoided. Instead, 1px horizontal hairline borders separate records.
- Faction affiliation is presented as a solid 2px vertical bar running down the extreme left edge of the competitor's row (Green: Syrtis, Red: Ignis, Blue: Alsius).
- Top 3 placements replace rank numbers with Roman numerals (I, II, III) finished in `#D4AF37`.

### Chips & Badges
- Strictly rectangular (`0px` radius).
- 1px hairline border surrounding black surfaces (`#050505`) with faction-colored text and matching 1px border. No pill containers.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#080808`, border `1px solid #1F1F1F`, font `JetBrains Mono` at `14px`.
- Active focus state sharpens the outline to `1px solid #D4AF37`. Error states switch the outline to `1px solid #EF4444`.

### Icons & Imagery
- Pure line-stroke medieval heraldry (swords, shields, battle axes, runic seals, and crown sigils).
- No emojis, cartoon graphics, or multicolored vector illustrations are permitted anywhere in the system.
